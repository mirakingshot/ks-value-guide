Searches every regular pack, the packs and shops of events running now, and the permanent event shops.

Packs are ranked by ratio: what everything inside is worth at regular pack rates, divided by the price. "Item share" is how much of that worth comes from the item you picked, so a high ratio with a small share means the deal is mostly other things. Shops are ranked by "% of best": how close the item comes to the best $ per token in that shop (or that tier of it), since tokens from different shops can't be compared directly. Event packs and shops only count while their event is running in the events tab; a pop-up with no event counts when its "available" column is marked.
