Variations of the same pack (named like "Pack - Option") are grouped into one row; click it to compare the options side by side. A base pack sets the value of the items marked base in it: the item gets the dollars in its "value" column, or, when that is blank, what is left of the pack price after the pack's other items are priced. That is why base packs sit close to 1.00x. Every other pack is valued with those item values.
