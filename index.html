About this guide

The purpose of this valuation guide is to help give a realistic reference to point to when special packs are a good deal and what items are most valuable in currency shops. The baseline value of items is determined by the most readily available and recurring packs for those items. For example, the baseline value for Truegold is determined by the value of TG in the Custom Selection recurring deal. If you are curious, the Masterlist has each item listed with its source pack associated with it.

Because item quantities in packs can vary by kingdom age, this guide likely will be off if you aren't in my kingdom range. Though, I believe it can still serve as a useful reference.
